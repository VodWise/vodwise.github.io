Frequently asked questions

The essentials

More questions? Visit the Help page.

When can I download it?

Not yet: the public download is under construction. When it’s ready, it will appear here and we’ll announce it in the community.

What do I need?

A PC with Windows 10 or 11, OBS to stream and record, and disk space for the recordings. No special computer needed.

Does it publish anything without me seeing it?

No. It prepares the videos and texts and you decide what goes out. Changing the title or moderating during the stream also needs your confirmation.

How much does it cost?

You pay with credits: there are monthly plans and top-ups that never expire, and you can cancel any time. The amounts are on Pricing.

Editing happens on your PC and nothing is published without your “yes”. Details in the privacy policy.
