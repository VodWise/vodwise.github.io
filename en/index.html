How it works

From recording to publishing, without editing all night

Vodwise works after the stream, on your computer, and leaves the result ready for you to review.

Stream as usual

Record your stream with OBS. While you are live, Vodwise can help by voice with titles, announcements and moderation, always with your confirmation.

Vodwise edits

When you finish, it transcribes what you say, finds the strongest moments and builds the full video, the highlights and the vertical clips.

Review and publish

Approve or discard each video, change whatever you want and publish. Vodwise learns from your decisions to get it right more often next time.

Your PC, your data

Your recordings never leave your computer

Vodwise is designed so that you are always in control.

  • Local editing. Video is processed and rendered on your own PC, with your graphics card.
  • Nothing is published without your approval. You review every video before it goes out.
  • Only your accounts. Vodwise acts only on your own channels.
  • Encrypted access. Authorizations are stored encrypted on your computer and you can revoke them at any time.
  • No ads, no tracking. Neither the app nor this website uses cookies.

What is sent out?

To write titles, pick moments and create thumbnails, Vodwise sends an artificial intelligence provider only what is strictly necessary: the text of what you say on stream and a few still images. The full recording is never uploaded.

Details are in the privacy policy.

FAQ

Common questions

What do I need to use Vodwise?

A Windows PC with a recent NVIDIA graphics card (editing uses the GPU to transcribe and render), OBS to record your streams and disk space for the recordings.

Which platforms does it work with?

Twitch, Kick and YouTube for streaming, and YouTube, TikTok, Instagram and X for publishing. Some automatic publishing depends on each platform approving the integration.

Does it publish anything without me seeing it?

No. Vodwise prepares the videos and the copy, and you decide what gets published. Actions during the stream, such as changing the title or moderating, also wait for your confirmation.

How long does it take to edit a stream?

It depends on the stream length and your hardware. Since it works after you finish streaming, it is usually ready the next day.

How much does it cost?

Vodwise is in development and has no price yet. If you join early access, you will be the first to know.
