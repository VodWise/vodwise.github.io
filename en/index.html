How it works

Before, during and after the stream

Vodwise installs on your PC, connects your accounts with one click and works with you through the whole stream.

Before

Gets the stream ready: title, category, thumbnail, YouTube broadcasts, Instagram story and tweet with one «yes». And a Stream Deck profile if you want one.

During

Unified chat, bot with commands and points, chat voices, OBS alerts and actions, voice moderation, Discord notices. Always with your confirmation when something is written to a platform.

After

Transcribes, picks the moments, builds the full video with chapters, the «best of» and subtitled Shorts, and leaves titles, description and thumbnail ready. You approve and publish.

Your PC, your data

Your recordings never leave your computer

  • Local editing. The video is processed on your own PC, with your graphics card.
  • Nothing is published without your approval. You review every video and every change before it goes out.
  • Only your accounts. Vodwise acts solely on your own channels.
  • Encrypted access. Authorizations are stored encrypted on your computer and you can revoke them any time.
  • No ads, no tracking. Neither the app nor this site uses cookies.

What is sent out?

To write titles, pick moments, talk and create thumbnails, Vodwise sends an AI provider only what is needed: the text of what you say and a few single frames; never the whole recording. Details in the privacy policy.

FAQ

Common questions

What do I need?

A Windows PC, OBS to stream and record, and disk space for the recording. An NVIDIA card is best for editing, but AMD, Intel or CPU-only also work (slower).

Which platforms?

Twitch, Kick and YouTube for the stream, and YouTube, TikTok, Instagram and X for publishing. One-click connection from the app.

Does it publish anything without me seeing it?

No. It prepares videos and texts and you decide what goes out. During the stream, changing the title or moderating also goes through your confirmation.

How much does it cost?

Nothing during the closed beta: every tester gets free credits. Later, plans from €14.99 a month with credits included and top-ups that never expire. Every feature records what it spends: you see it in the app.

Is it safe to install?

The beta is not code-signed yet: Windows shows «Windows protected your PC»; click «More info» → «Run anyway». The app never touches OBS or your platforms without your permission.
