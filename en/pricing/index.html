Quality
What does each quality include?

Basic: copilot, long-form video, subtitles, titles and thumbnail. Default (what the app comes with): also the stream's best moments, 6 Shorts and an English version. Maximum: also 8 reviewed Shorts, premium editing and a high-quality thumbnail.

Questions

Common questions

What is a credit?

The way your usage is measured. Every time the AI works for you (editing a stream, writing a title, talking with you live) it uses a few credits. The app shows your balance and you can choose features and quality to spend less.

Do credits expire?

Top-up credits, never. Plan credits renew every month; on Premium and Studio, unused credits roll over up to 2 and 3 months.

What if I run out of credits in the middle of an edit?

Whatever started finishes: it's never cut halfway. Nothing new starts until you top up or your next month begins.

Can I cancel anytime?

Yes, whenever you want. You keep your plan until the end of the paid month and you keep your top-up credits.

What does the free version include?

Everything that doesn't need artificial intelligence: Twitch and Kick chat, VodwiseBot, alerts, actions, OBS overlays, Windows chat voices and a few basic clips of every stream made on your PC. It carries a discreet Vodwise signature that goes away with any plan.
