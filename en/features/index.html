Features

Everything a stream needs: multistream chat, bot, alerts and automatic editing

Vodwise brings together in one Windows app what a streamer uses before, during and after the stream: a copilot you can talk to, the Twitch, Kick and YouTube chat and bot, OBS alerts and scenes, and editing of the stream on your own PC. What used to take five programs and a night of editing.

Your account

Live

Your copilot during the stream

Talk to Vodwise like a person: «what is my title?», «switch to the chat scene», «set the category to Minecraft». It prepares the change, shows it per platform and applies it when you say «yes». Next to it, your platforms with title, category and viewers, the stream activity and what moderation did.

Live

First steps

The first time, the assistant guides you by voice or text: your name, your account, what to prepare, your platforms, OBS and your style. Leave it halfway and continue another day.

Live

Live voice (F8)

If you want, the assistant listens to the stream and only joins when you talk to it, name it or the chat deserves it. Its voice goes to the stream, in an economy or a more natural mode.

Live

Talk to the assistant (F9)

Press a key, talk and release, like a walkie-talkie: ask, request a scene or title change, or a search. It answers in the app and, if you like, out loud.

Live

Stream summary and status

«How is the stream going?» gives you a diary of what happened: raids, subs, moments, audience. Your viewers can ask for it in chat. And a Status page tells you what is connected and what is not.

Chat

Unified chat

Twitch, Kick and YouTube in one window, with emotes, badges, highlights and one-click moderation from the name.

Chat

Multistream bot

Commands, timers, thanks by type, points and levels and spam filters. The same bot on all three platforms, with switches and cooldowns per command.

Chat

Chat voices

Chat messages are read out on stream, each viewer with a fixed voice. Speed and voices your way; the moderation filter runs first.

Chat

Actions

Point rewards, commands with a cost, bits and KICKs that trigger sounds, images and videos in OBS. Import the ones you have or create new ones.

Chat

Discord

Vodwise posts on your server like a person: when you start, about the good moments (raids, gifts, hype trains) and when you end. And it chats with your community in the channels you choose.

Screen and OBS

Alerts

Follows, subs, gifts, bits, raids and redemptions with alerts per platform and type, volume-matched. Use yours or ask the AI for a full set with your logo.

Screen and OBS

Overlays your way

A layer editor for your overlays: texts, shapes, images, latest follow, goals, on-screen chat and viewers. Or describe what you want and the AI builds it.

Screen and OBS

Scenes and design

Full scene collections (start, chat, game, break, end) with their own transition and your logo as the emblem, designed by the AI from your style, with matching end credits.

Screen and OBS

OBS setup

Analyzes your PC and connection, proposes the right quality, builds scenes, profiles and stream keys, installs what is missing and reviews your OBS. Yours is untouched: everything goes into a new profile.

Screen and OBS

End credits

When you finish, credits with everyone who came by the chat, the subs, raids and donations. Pick a ready-made design, tweak it or ask the AI for a new one.

Screen and OBS

VTuber avatar

Instead of your camera, a 2D avatar, made from your own images or created with AI. It speaks with your mic and blinks on its own. If you turn on “Follow my face”, it also follows your mouth with your webcam. Your camera image never leaves your PC.

Screen and OBS

Stream Deck

A profile for your Stream Deck with your OBS scenes, the music, the assistant keys and the Twitch actions, arranged by the AI. Imported with a double click.

Editing

Full video and best-of

When the stream ends, Vodwise reviews the whole thing, trims silences, picks the moments and builds the full video with chapters and subtitles, plus an 8–13 minute «best of». You review it in the app, video by video.

Editing

Subtitled Shorts

4–6 vertical clips per stream, with your camera tracked, a headline and subtitles. With a publishing calendar (one a day) and the option to ask for another moment.

Editing

Titles, description and thumbnail

Catchy titles and a description built for what people search now, tags, chapters and several thumbnails with your real face to test which works best.

Editing

Publish in one click

Once you approve the edit, a page with everything ready to copy (titles, descriptions, Shorts, TikTok and Instagram texts), the files folder and direct access to YouTube Studio, TikTok Studio and Instagram.

Editing

My style

Tell Vodwise how you want your titles, thumbnails, editing pace and subtitles, in your own words. It uses it in every edit: two streamers never get the same thing.

Your account

Credits for real use

Pay for what you use: every feature records its cost and you see it by category. Plans with credits included and top-ups that never expire.

Your account

Features and spending

Choose how much Vodwise does with each stream (saving, balanced or maximum) or switch each feature separately, with an estimate of what it will spend.

Your account

Your own donations

Coming soon: your donation page with your name, collected in your own Stripe or PayPal account, with name and message on stream.

Your account

Statistics

Viewers, follows, subs and events per stream and per platform, in the app.

Your account

Your PC, your data

The recording never leaves your computer; authorizations are stored encrypted and you revoke them any time; nothing is published without your approval.

Your account

Hassle-free install

One installer for Windows. What editing needs is downloaded once, every file verified, and updates arrive by themselves.
