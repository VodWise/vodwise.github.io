Features

Multistream chat, bot, alerts and automatic editing, in one app

Vodwise brings together, in one Windows app, what you use before, during and after your stream. These are its eight areas; the detail of each one is in the help.

  • Unified chat

    Twitch, Kick and YouTube in a single window, with badges and emotes.

    You reply on all three platforms and moderate with one click from the username.

    More detail in the help →

  • Moderation and bot

    Commands, timers, thank-yous and spam filters.

    The same bot on all three platforms, with a switch for each command.

    More detail in the help →

  • Alerts and OBS

    Animated alerts by type and platform, overlays and scenes made to measure.

    Vodwise helps you set up OBS and works with Stream Deck and button boxes.

    More detail in the help →

  • Voice and copilot

    You talk to Vodwise like you would to a person: change scene, title or category.

    It prepares the change, shows it to you and applies it when you say “yes”.

    More detail in the help →

  • Automatic editing and Shorts

    When the stream ends, Vodwise trims silences, picks the moments and builds the video with chapters and subtitles.

    Between 4 and 6 vertical Shorts per stream, no watermark.

    More detail in the help →

  • Packs and AI design

    Describe your style and the AI proposes scenes, alerts and overlays that match your logo.

    The ready-to-use packs: Under construction

    More detail in the help →

  • Publishing

    YouTube: the video goes up as private and you review the video and its texts before publishing it.

    TikTok and Instagram: Under construction

    More detail in the help →

  • Account, credits and privacy

    You pay with credits for what you use, and top-ups never expire.

    Your recording stays on your PC and nothing is published without your approval.

    More detail in the help →
