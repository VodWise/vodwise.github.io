Getting started

  1. Download Vodwise for Windows. Under construction
  2. Sign in with your Twitch or Kick account (Sign in).
  3. Connect your platforms and OBS in one click.
  4. Do your first stream. Nothing goes out without your permission.

Frequently asked questions

How do I install Vodwise?

When it’s available, you’ll download it from the download page and open it. If Windows shows “Windows protected your PC”, click “More info” and “Run anyway”.

How do I connect Twitch, Kick, YouTube and OBS?

From the app, with one click each.

Does it publish anything without me seeing it?

No. It prepares the videos and texts and you decide what goes out. During the stream, changing the title or moderating also needs your confirmation.

How much does it cost and what are credits?

Everything without AI is free. AI features use credits and each one records what it spends: you can see it in the app. Top-ups don’t expire.

Plans and top-ups are bought from the app (Settings → Account) and charged by Link (Stripe), which issues the invoice. See the plans.

I can’t see my chat or my stream in the app

Check in Settings that the platform is connected and that OBS is open.
