Questions

Common questions

What is a credit?

The way your usage is measured. Every time the AI works for you (editing a stream, writing a title, talking with you live) it uses a few credits. The app shows your balance and how many streams you have left, and you can choose features and quality to spend less.

What if I run out of credits in the middle of an edit?

Whatever started finishes: it's never cut halfway. Nothing new starts until you top up or your next month begins.

Do credits expire?

Top-up credits, never. Plan credits renew every month; on Pro and Elite, unused credits roll over up to 2 and 3 months.

Can I cancel anytime?

Yes, whenever you want. You keep your plan until the end of the paid month and you keep your top-up credits.

Do I need to enter keys or create apps?

No. You sign in with Twitch or Google and connect each platform with one button. No keys, nothing technical to set up.

Are my recordings uploaded anywhere?

Your recordings stay on your computer. Only what the AI needs to work is sent, and it isn't stored. Nothing is published without your "yes". More details in the privacy policy.

Which platforms does it work with?

Twitch, YouTube and Kick for your streams; YouTube, TikTok and Instagram for your videos and clips, and X to announce you're live.

Can I delete my account?

Yes, from the app itself: your subscription is canceled and your account, balance and history are deleted.
