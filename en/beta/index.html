Vodwise free beta: request your invite

Vodwise is in a closed beta with limited spots. Leave your email and confirm it: when a spot opens, you get your personal invite.

  1. Join with your email and click the confirmation link we send you (check spam too).
  2. When a spot opens, you get a personal link: accept the beta agreement and download the installer (if Windows warns, «More info» → «Run anyway»).
  3. Sign in with Twitch or Kick, request your credits from the app, connect your platforms and OBS with one click and do your first stream with Vodwise.

FAQ

Common questions

What do I need?

A Windows PC, OBS to stream and record, and disk space for the recording. No special computer needed.

Which platforms?

Twitch, Kick and YouTube for the stream, and YouTube, TikTok, Instagram and X for publishing. One-click connection from the app.

Does it publish anything without me seeing it?

No. It prepares videos and texts and you decide what goes out. During the stream, changing the title or moderating also goes through your confirmation.

How much does it cost?

During the closed beta you can use everything that doesn’t involve AI for free; the paid AI features are coming very soon. Later, plans from €14.99 a month with credits included and top-ups that never expire. Every feature records what it spends: you see it in the app.

Is it safe to install?

The beta is not code-signed yet: Windows shows «Windows protected your PC»; click «More info» → «Run anyway». The app never touches OBS or your platforms without your permission.
