Vodwise compared with Opus Clip, Eklipse, StreamLadder, StreamElements and Streamlabs

  • Opus Clip

    What it does. Turns a long video into vertical clips with animated subtitles and a viral-potential score. You paste a link or upload a file.

    Where it wins. It runs in the browser, on Mac too, and works for any video, not only streams.

    Where Vodwise is different. Vodwise is made for streamers and works with your OBS recording, on your PC, without uploading anything.

  • Eklipse

    What it does. Connects to your Twitch, Kick or YouTube channel, finds the best moments and turns them into vertical clips with subtitles for TikTok, Shorts and Reels.

    Where it wins. Nothing to install, it can work with the stream saved on your channel even if you did not record, and it schedules publishing.

    Where Vodwise is different. Besides clips, it is with you during the stream (chat, bot, alerts) and prepares the long video for YouTube.

  • StreamLadder

    What it does. A browser editor: you paste the link of a Twitch clip, pick a camera-and-game template, add subtitles and download it or schedule it on TikTok. Its AI, ClipGPT, finds moments in a full stream.

    Where it wins. Full control over every clip, a free plan for the editor and a mobile app.

    Where Vodwise is different. Vodwise proposes the moments when the stream ends and you approve them, without hunting for clips or pasting links.

  • StreamElements and Streamlabs

    What it does. Alerts, overlays, chat bot and tips. StreamElements works in the cloud and is added to OBS; Streamlabs has its own program, Streamlabs Desktop. Razer bought StreamElements on 31 July 2026 and it keeps working.

    Where it wins. Thousands of templates and tutorials, and the basics are free. Streamlabs Desktop runs on Windows and Mac, supports Kick and includes Replay, which cuts automatic clips for dozens of games (Windows only).

    Where Vodwise is different. One bot with the same commands on Twitch, Kick and YouTube, a unified chat where you moderate from the same window and, when the stream ends, the video. Vodwise does not send your stream to several platforms: for that you still need a multistream service.
